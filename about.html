About Beneficial Backyards

Beneficial Backyards is a New Zealand landscaping business focused on residential outdoor spaces.

Who we are

We help homeowners shape and care for their backyards and gardens. The business currently spans four branches: residential landscaping, small digger work, gardening, and garden sculptures made from corten steel.

Whether you need practical groundwork, ongoing garden care, or a corten steel piece for the garden, Beneficial Backyards brings those strands together under one name.

How we work

Residential landscaping

Outdoor spaces for homes — planning and carrying out landscaping that suits how you live outside.

Hands-on garden work

Small digger work and gardening for clearing, shaping, planting, and maintaining outdoor areas.

Corten steel sculptures

Garden sculptures in corten steel — weathering steel pieces designed for outdoor display.

Getting started

Email info@beneficialbackyards.co.nz or use the contact form with a short note about your project — we’ll follow up to discuss what’s needed.
